Components compiled into the WebAssembly of @mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.0.1
(wasm/vision_wasm_internal.wasm, vision_wasm_nosimd_internal.wasm and
vision_wasm_module_internal.wasm, bundled with EdiAula to run the webcam
background-removal model locally)

is built together with the libraries below; their names and symbols appear in
the WebAssembly files. Each keeps its own license.
