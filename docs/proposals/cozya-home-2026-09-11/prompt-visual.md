## Prompt de geração

Create one exceptionally polished high-fidelity art-direction concept for the Brazilian home-cooking web app COZYA's sales homepage. This is a DESIGN PROPOSAL of the desktop hero and the beginning of the next section, not a wireframe, not a poster. Landscape canvas approximately 1600 x 1200 pixels, crisp readable web typography. No browser chrome and no device frames.

BRAND AND ART DIRECTION: warm ivory paper background #FFF8EE, very dark navy typography #173247, deliberate appetizing orange #FF7A3D for the principal action, sparing olive-green functional checkmarks. Sophisticated contemporary food editorial meets beautifully usable software. Large bold geometric sans-serif headline resembling Plus Jakarta Sans, editorial whitespace, oversized typography, clean asymmetrical grid. Authentic appetizing natural-light photography, not illustration, no purple gradients, no glassmorphism, no floating abstract blobs, no stock smiling people. Quiet soft shadows only on one functional product panel.

LAYOUT: slim navigation across the top, small orange wordmark 'cozya' at left, three subdued links 'Como funciona', 'Planos', 'Entrar' toward the right. Wide generous margins. Hero left column about 48% width: a restrained eyebrow 'SUA SEMANA À MESA'; an arrestingly large navy headline broken across 3-4 lines, exact Portuguese text 'Seu jantar resolvido antes de a fome chegar.' Underneath, short readable paragraph 'Escolha suas receitas, planeje jantares e marmitas e leve a lista pronta ao mercado.' Large orange rounded-rectangle button with dark navy text 'Planejar minha semana grátis'. Below, small two-line reassurance '14 dias de planejamento + 3 créditos de IA' and 'Sem cartão. Sem renovação automática.' A quiet text link 'Explorar uma semana de exemplo' appears beneath.

HERO RIGHT: large inviting editorial photograph occupying most of the right column, softly rounded upper corners, cream ceramic plate of penne pasta with chopped tomatoes and chickpeas, natural olive oil shine and herbs, linen tablecloth, casually placed fork, a small glass food container beside the plate subtly suggesting tomorrow's lunchbox, late-afternoon sunshine and refined lived-in Brazilian home feel. Picture extends vertically from below the navigation to near the main CTA baseline. Overlay ONLY ONE large, clearly readable realistic Cozya app panel over the bottom part of the photograph, in white with restrained rounded corners and a tiny orange accent line. It is a weekly meal-plan panel with small title 'Minha semana', mini navigation 'Receitas   Semana   Compras', and three tidy meal rows:
'SEG · Jantar' / 'Macarrão com tomate e grão-de-bico' / '4 porções'
'TER · Jantar' / 'Omelete de abobrinha' / '2 porções'
'QUA · Marmita' / 'Arroz com lentilha e cenoura' / '4 porções'.
A navy full-width action in this panel says 'Gerar lista de compras'.
The photo must remain visible above the panel. Don't use food thumbnails inside the panel. No unimplemented AI auto-planning suggestions, voice chat, nutrition scores, smart fridge features or fake financial savings.

BOTTOM START OF SECOND SECTION, clearly separated by generous whitespace and a thin horizontal divider: left small text 'VEJA A ROTINA ACONTECER', a strong smaller headline 'Da receita salva à mesa posta.' and one short line 'Planeje. Compre. Cozinhe. Repita o que funcionou.' Right a horizontal understated product step strip with three readable items '01 Sua semana' then '02 Suas compras' then '03 Na cozinha', connected with subtle arrows, the first selected in pale orange. A small visible label 'Exemplo de uso' makes clear these are illustrative data.
No testimonials, fake users, stars, rating badges, counters, invented guarantees or pricing. No paragraph filler, no lorem ipsum, no words other than the specified Portuguese copy. Preserve accents. The final image should feel warm, premium, intensely appetizing, useful and commercially persuasive, with the actual weekly cooking workflow unmistakably more prominent than AI.
